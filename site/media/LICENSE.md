# MyTube original media

All SVG thumbnails, animated MP4 clips and synthesized soundtracks generated by
`scripts/generate-media.py` are original procedural works for this repository.
No third-party footage, photographs, samples, music recordings or AI video models
are used. Audio is mathematically synthesized; scenes are drawn in Python.

To the extent possible under law, these original media are dedicated to the
public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You may copy, modify and redistribute the media commercially without attribution.
This dedication does not apply to application dependencies. ffmpeg/libx264's
software licenses do not impose licensing on the original video outputs.
